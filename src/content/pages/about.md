Jacob Spetzler is a photographer, journalist and educator from Gunnison, Colorado. He is a Lecturer in Filmmaking & Photography at Western Colorado University. He earned his MFA from the University of Hartford International Limited Residency Photography Program in 2025. Previously he was the Photo and Sports Editor at the Gunnison Country Times.

Editorial clients include Associated Press, The Washington Post, The Denver Post, Colorado Public Radio, The Crested Butte News, The Gunnison Country Times and The Tico Times, among others.
