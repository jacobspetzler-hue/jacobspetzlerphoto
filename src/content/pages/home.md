Photographer and filmmaker based in Gunnison, Colorado. His editorial work focuses on land, water and fire in the American West and appears in The Denver Post, The Washington Post and with the Associated Press.

He holds an MFA in photography from the Hartford Art School and teaches film and photography at Western Colorado University.
