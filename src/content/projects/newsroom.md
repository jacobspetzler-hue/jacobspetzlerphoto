NEWSROOM is a handmade photobook composed of photographs made in small-town local media offices and print presses in central Colorado. The images are mainly interior still lifes, many of which verge on complete abstraction. The work explores questions around literal and figurative collective memory, the role local news plays in creating a shared identity for a community, durability of archival mediums and the ways in which the fundamental act of creation is tied to the earliest forms of human expression.
